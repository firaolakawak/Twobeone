This preview loads the repository's font stylesheet. Noto supplies Ethiopic glyphs; Latin and Oromo use platform fonts. The strategy is shared with the app. This specimen is not evidence of testing on every operating system.

  • Use weight 400 for reading, 500 for captions, 600 for labels and card titles, and 700 for page and section titles.
  • Keep normal letter spacing for body text and labels. Reserve slight tightening for large Latin headings; use normal spacing for Amharic.
  • Keep body text and fields at 16px with 1.5 line-height; supporting copy at 14px and captions at 12px.
  • Keep longer Scripture and reading views comfortably spaced as an explicit reading variant.
